Real-world photos

Clients rarely have studio pictures. That is fine.

A local AI model cuts the product out of an ordinary phone photo in about five seconds. The result goes through the same brand kits and templates as any other picture.

Photos: two public-domain (CC0) images from Wikimedia Commons, listed in assets/cutout/CREDITS.txt. Tested the same way on headphones, earbuds with thin cables, and a phone in a glossy clear case.

Made for you

How we tailor it to your brand.

  1. You send the basics

    Logo, brand colours and fonts, or your brand guidelines. Five to ten products with whatever photos you have. The languages and formats you post in.

  2. We build your kit

    Your brand file, your product files and clean cut-outs from your photos, checked on every template.

  3. You choose the looks

    A contact sheet shows your own products in every style. You pick the ones that feel like you, or ask for something new.

  4. Posts start flowing

    Posts, reels and carousels from your catalog, in your look, in batches your team reviews before anything goes live.
