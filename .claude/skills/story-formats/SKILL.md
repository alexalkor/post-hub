# Story formats (9:16)

Brand rules, facts and approvals come from the project. If the project has a post studio, render stories with it at 1080x1920 and leave the sticker area empty: **stickers (poll, quiz, link, countdown) are added by hand in the Instagram app**, because exported images can't contain live stickers.

## Formats
| Format | Use | Frame layout | Sticker area |
|---|---|---|---|
| Poll / this-or-that | opinion, light engagement | question top third, 2 products or options mid | lower middle, about 600x250 px |
| Quiz | teach a fact | question plus 3 to 4 answers drawn as space | middle; correct answer from checked facts |
| Emoji slider | feeling, "how much do you want..." | big product, short line | below the product |
| Question box | collect DMs and FAQs | "Ask us about X" | middle |
| Countdown | launch, event, offer end | big date or teaser image | middle |
| Link | catalogue, site, form | product plus 1 benefit plus "Tap the link" | lower third, above the bottom 340 px |
| Product teaser | new arrival | close-up crop, reveal on frame 2 | none |

## Sequences (3 to 5 frames)
- **Teach**: hook frame, then fact, then quiz, then answer plus link.
- **Launch**: teaser, then reveal, then 3 benefits, then countdown or link.
- **Choose**: problem, then 2 options (poll), then which fits whom, then link to catalogue.
Same background family and type across the sequence. Each frame is readable in 3 s.

## Layout rules
- Safe zone: nothing important in the top 250 px or the bottom 340 px.
- Text large: title 80 to 110 px, body at least 44 px. At most 2 lines of body per frame.
- Motion (if video): 5 to 7 s per frame; follow the `motion-design` skill.
- Deliver per frame: PNG or MP4, plus a note listing which sticker goes where and its exact text, in the account's language.
