# What we need from you

A short checklist so we can make posts that look like your brand. A first answer is enough. Photos do not have to be perfect: we can work from ordinary phone pictures.

## 1. Your brand
- [ ] **Logo**: an SVG, or the largest PNG you have. A screenshot is not enough.
- [ ] **Colours**: your brand guidelines, or the colour codes (for example #1F5BFF) for the main and the second brand colour, the background and the text.
- [ ] **Fonts**: the names of the fonts you use for headlines and for text, if you have them.
- [ ] **Voice**: three words that describe how you sound (for example calm, direct, playful), and any words you always or never use.
- [ ] **Posts you like**: up to three posts, yours or from others, that feel right. And one you would never want.

## 2. Your products
Start with 5 to 10. For each one:
- [ ] The **name** as customers know it.
- [ ] **Three key facts** (short) and **four specifications** (for example size, material, power, colours). We only use what you give us in writing.
- [ ] **Photos**: any you have. Studio shots are best, phone photos are fine. A plain background helps but is not required. Please send the largest files, not screenshots.
- [ ] Anything you do not want mentioned (prices, claims, competitors).

## 3. Where you post
- [ ] **Platforms and accounts**, and how many accounts per language.
- [ ] **Languages** for the texts.
- [ ] **Formats**: feed post, square, story or reel, carousel.
- [ ] **How often** you would like to post.

## 4. Rules
- [ ] Words, claims or topics that need approval or are not allowed in your industry.
- [ ] Whether people may appear in pictures, and whether you have their agreement.
- [ ] Who approves posts on your side, and how quickly.

## What happens next
1. We build your brand file and your product files, and prepare your photos.
2. You see your own products in several styles and pick the ones you like.
3. We make a first set of posts, reels and a carousel for your review.
4. You approve, and we keep the same look for everything that follows.

Nothing is published without your approval.
